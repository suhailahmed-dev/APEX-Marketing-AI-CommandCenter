## 🎯 What it does

When the business owner or client opens the dashboard, they can immediately inspect **"Kaam Kaise Hua Hai"** (work history, stats, live deliverables feed) and execute any of the 15 specialized marketing agents with 1 click:

### 📱 1. Social & Creative Suite
* **Social Media Content**: Viral hooks, captions, and hashtag clusters for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter/X.
* **Content Calendar**: 7, 15, or 30-day multi-channel content roadmaps formatted as clean tables with CTAs.
* **Post & Reel Ideas Lab**: Niche-tailored short-form reel hooks, carousels, and story arcs.

### ✍️ 2. Copy & Conversion
* **Copywriting Engine**: Sales letters, landing page hero sections, VSL scripts, and PAS/AIDA frameworks.
* **E-Commerce Product Content**: Amazon/Shopify titles, sensory descriptions, bullet benefits, and FAQ accordions.

### 🔍 3. SEO & Organic Traffic
* **On-Page SEO Architect**: High-CTR Title tags, click-magnet Meta descriptions, H1/H2 hierarchy, and schema FAQs.
* **Keyword Research & Clusters**: Seed keyword expansion into 4 topical clusters with search intent classification.
* **Full SEO Blog Generator**: 1,500+ word human-like, publish-ready articles with key takeaways and FAQ sections.
* **SEO Audit & Health Report**: Executive site health diagnosis, priority action matrix, and 90-day organic recovery roadmap.

### 🎯 4. Advertising & Sales
* **High-ROAS Ad Studio**: Facebook & Instagram ad copies, thumb-stopping hooks, audience targeting recommendations, and creative briefs.
* **Email Marketing Sequences**: Promotional flash sales, 3-part welcome series, weekly newsletters, and cart recovery sequences.

### 📋 5. Strategy & Client Operations
* **360° Marketing Strategy**: Complete GTM blueprint, ICP personas, funnel architecture, channel breakdown, and budget allocation.
* **Competitor Intelligence & Gaps**: Competitor teardown, positioning comparison table, and market gaps to exploit.
* **Client Communications & Proposals**: Monthly client win reports, proposal summaries, and milestone handovers.
* **Executive Marketing Report**: Formatted KPI scorecard, wins analysis, and strategic roadmap for business owners.

Double click `open_dashboard.bat`. It will launch the local server and open `http://localhost:8800` directly in your browser.

## 🏢 Client & Brand Customization
Click the **Brand Profile** badge at the bottom of the sidebar or top bar to customize:
* Company Name
* Industry & Niche
* Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
* Brand Voice & Tone
* Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

Every single AI agent automatically reads this profile, ensuring every post, ad, and strategy is 100% personalized to your client's business!
